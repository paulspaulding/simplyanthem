# Anthem Home Sales in September 2026

Anthem recorded 49 closed sales in September 2026. The median sale price was $620,000, with sales ranging from about $380,000 to over $3 million.

## What the Numbers Show

- **49 closed sales** across Anthem in September
- **Median sale price:** $620,000
- **Most sales** fell between $400,000 and $650,000
- **Higher-end sales** above $1 million lifted the average

## What It Means For You

**Buyers:** Anthem offers a range of homes, so it helps to know which price band fits your budget and which sales have recently closed nearby.

**Sellers:** Recent sales show what buyers are paying. Pricing against the closed sales in your area matters more than the asking price of other listings.

Closed sales data from the Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS (Agent Single Line report), September 2026. Figures are approximate and for informational purposes only.
