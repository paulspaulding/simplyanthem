# October 26 Las Vegas Housing Update

The Las Vegas REALTORS® Monthly Housing Market Update for September 2026 is out, and the headline is steady prices alongside a bigger selection of homes. Single-family median prices held at $470,000, unchanged from last year, even as more homes came on the market. Below you'll also find a quick look at Anthem, plus what's happening around Henderson and the Strip this fall.

## The Headlines

**Single-family homes**
- 1,745 units sold, down 7.4% from last year
- 53.4% sold in less than 30 days
- Median price: $470,000, flat (0.0%)
- Units available: 7,995, up 6.6%
- Effective availability: 4.6 months, up 15.1%

**Condos and townhouses**
- 424 units sold, down 12.6% from last year
- 47.2% sold in less than 30 days
- Median price: $290,000, down 1.4%
- Units available: 2,796, up 7.3%
- Effective availability: 6.6 months, up 22.8%

## What Stands Out

- **Single-family prices are holding.** More inventory has not pushed the median down.
- **Condo and townhouse buyers have more choice.** Effective availability rose 22.8% and the median price slipped 1.4%.
- **Fewer sales, more listings.** Units sold fell year over year while available units grew, so buyers have more time to compare.

## Anthem Snapshot

Anthem recorded 49 closed sales in September, with a median sale price of $620,000. Most sales landed between $400,000 and $650,000, and a handful of higher-end sales above $1 million pulled the average up. Recent closings are the best guide to what buyers are paying nearby, so check them before you set a list price or make an offer.

## What It Means For You

**Buyers:** More homes to choose from means you can be selective. Ask about price history on listings that have sat for a while.

**Sellers:** With nearly 8,000 single-family homes available, price strategy matters. About half of single-family homes sold within 30 days in September, so a well-priced home still moves.

**Investors:** Rising supply can open doors, but run the numbers on rents and carrying costs before you buy.

## Things to Do in Henderson This Season

- **Trails and parks:** The River Mountains Loop Trail is free for running and cycling, and Cornerstone, Acacia, and Anthem Hills parks are free to visit.
- **Birdwatching:** The Henderson Bird Viewing Preserve is free and a good weekend walk.
- **Lake Las Vegas:** Waterfront dining, boating, and resort events are a short drive from Anthem.
- **Watch parties:** The City of Henderson is hosting an F1 Watch Party on November 21.
- **Winter events:** WinterFest runs December 3 and 4.
