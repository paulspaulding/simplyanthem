# The Numbers Behind the Noise

The latest Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS update gives Southern Nevada buyers, sellers, and investors a clearer view beneath the headlines: prices are holding while inventory continues to rise.

## Single-Family Homes

- 1,745 units sold, down 7.4% from the prior year
- 53.4% sold in fewer than 30 days
- Median price: $470,000, unchanged year over year
- 7,995 units available, up 6.6%
- 4.6 months of effective availability, up 15.1%

Single-family prices are holding their ground even as more homes come to market. That combination points to a market with real demand, but less urgency than during the peak seller-driven years. Buyers have more time to compare homes and negotiate, while well-priced properties can still move quickly.

## Condos and Townhouses

- 424 units sold, down 12.6% from the prior year
- 47.2% sold in fewer than 30 days
- Median price: $290,000, down 1.4%
- 2,796 units available, up 7.3%
- 6.6 months of effective availability, up 22.8%

The condo and townhouse market is giving buyers even more options. A longer supply of 6.6 months creates room to compare condition, location, HOA costs, and price before making a decision. Sellers in this segment should expect buyers to be selective and pricing to matter.

## What the Numbers Mean

- **Prices are stable.** The single-family median is unchanged at $470,000, while the condo median is $290,000.
- **Inventory is rising.** Available single-family homes increased to 7,995 units, and condo inventory reached 2,796 units.
- **Buyer leverage is growing.** Effective availability rose to 4.6 months for single-family homes and 6.6 months for condos.
- **Demand is still present.** More than half of single-family homes sold within 30 days, showing that buyers continue to act when a property is correctly priced.

## What It Means For You

**Buyers:** More inventory means more room to compare, inspect, and negotiate. Condos and townhouses offer the strongest selection and affordability advantage, while single-family buyers benefit from a wider range of choices than they had a year ago.

**Sellers:** A rising supply of competing listings makes pricing and presentation more important. A well-prepared, accurately priced home can still stand out, but sellers should not rely on the momentum of the 2021–2022 market.

**Investors:** Stable prices and expanding inventory create a broader set of properties to evaluate. Compare purchase price, rents, HOA costs, insurance, taxes, and carrying costs before making a decision.

## The Bottom Line

The numbers behind the noise point to a normalizing Southern Nevada housing market. Prices are not falling dramatically, demand has not disappeared, and inventory is giving buyers more leverage. The market is becoming more deliberate: informed buyers have time, prepared sellers can still compete, and investors have more opportunities to underwrite carefully.

## Source and Methodology

Data from the Las Vegas REALTORS® Monthly Housing Market Update for September 2026. Growth rates shown in the report are compared with the prior year. The report covers the Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS market and should be used as general market information, not as individualized financial or investment advice.
