# September 2026 Las Vegas Housing Market Update

Single-family prices held steady in September while more homes came on the market. Condos and townhouses saw softer prices and more choices for buyers. Here's what the latest Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS numbers show.

## The Headlines

**Single-family homes**
- 1,745 homes sold, down 7.4% from last year
- 53.4% sold within 30 days
- Median price: $470,000, unchanged from last year
- Units available: 7,995, up 6.6%
- Effective availability: 4.6 months, up 15.1%

**Condos and townhouses**
- 424 units sold, down 12.6% from last year
- 47.2% sold within 30 days
- Median price: $290,000, down 1.4%
- Units available: 2,796, up 7.3%
- Effective availability: 6.6 months, up 22.8%

## What the Numbers Say

- **Single-family prices are holding.** Even with more homes for sale, the median price didn't move from last year.
- **Condos have more choices.** Higher inventory and a longer supply give buyers more room to compare options.
- **Buyers have more negotiating room.** With more listings available, there's more time to make a decision.

## What It Means For You

**Buyers:** More inventory means more options. Focus on homes that have been on the market a while, and ask about recent price changes.

**Sellers:** Pricing matters more when there are more listings to compete with. A well-priced home still sells quickly, and about half of single-family homes sold within 30 days this month.

**Investors:** Rising supply can create opportunities, but compare rents and carrying costs carefully before buying.

## Source

Data from the Las Vegas REALTORS® Monthly Housing Market Update, September 2026. Growth rates are compared to the prior year.
